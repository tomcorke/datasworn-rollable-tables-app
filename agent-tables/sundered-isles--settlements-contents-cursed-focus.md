# Cursed Settlement Focus
- **Ruleset:** sundered_isles
- **Collection:** Settlement Oracles
- **Table ID:** `sundered_isles/settlements/contents/cursed_focus`
- **Source data:** `src/data/sundered_isles-settlements.yaml`
## Description

If you roll a [Settlement Focus](id:sundered_isles/collections/settlements/focus) curse, take your result on that table and make note that it is cursed. Something about this activity has an uncanny or dreadful aspect. If you investigate or interact further, use the table below to help reveal the industry’s relationship to a cursed power.
## Results
### 1-10

Acting to fulfill a dreadful bargain


### 11-20

Bound to the service of a cursed power


### 21-30

Corrupted or sickened by the effects of a cursed location or relic


### 31-40

Desperately working in defiance of a cursed power


### 41-50

Enduring dreadful conditions


### 51-60

Granted unusual abilities or resources by a cursed power


### 61-70

In zealous service of a cursed power


### 71-80

Lost lives or resources under the deprivations of a cursed power


### 81-90

Targeted by a cursed power


### 91-100

Unwittingly helping or unleashing a cursed power
