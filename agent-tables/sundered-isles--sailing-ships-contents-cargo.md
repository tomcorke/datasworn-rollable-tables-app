- **Ruleset:** sundered_isles

- **Source data:** `src/data/sundered_isles-sailing_ships.yaml`
## Description

Use this table when surveying a ship’s hold or if coming upon lost or abandoned cargo. Roll or choose a number of items as appropriate to the nature of the find. For example, once for the hold of a typical warship, or up to four times for a fully loaded tradeship. Ships also typically carry basic munitions and provisions for the crew, but this table reveals the details of cargo such as trade goods and plunder.
