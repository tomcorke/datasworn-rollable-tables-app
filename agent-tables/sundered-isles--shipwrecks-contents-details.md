# Shipwreck Details
- **Ruleset:** sundered_isles
- **Collection:** Shipwreck Oracles

- **Source data:** `src/data/sundered_isles-shipwrecks.yaml`
## Description

Use this table to reveal details of a wreck. Roll once or twice when searching a small ship, and up to three times when surveying a large vessel. These details are revealed in the context of another move—such as Explore a Waypoint or Gather Information—but might prompt additional actions or investigations.
