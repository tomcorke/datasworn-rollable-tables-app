## Description

Use this table to reveal characteristics of a treasure or its surroundings. Since the results often introduce twists and complications, it is best reserved for notable finds and dramatic situations. Also, keep in mind that an aspect might be outside your character’s immediate viewpoint—if so, make note of it as a future trouble or opportunity.
